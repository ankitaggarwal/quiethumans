bottleneck 4 · living on one machine

local is free. local is also a leash.

everything runs on the same Mac, so everything competes, and you babysit it.

one box, everything on it

Qdrant, Postgres, two models and the crawler all fight for the same RAM and CPU.

you can't close the lid

it's your laptop. a reboot, a sleep, an update, and the overnight crawl is gone.

slow, and hard to scale

no rate limits, but bounded throughput. you can't just "add a bigger box."

great for building and demoing. awkward to run always-on.
